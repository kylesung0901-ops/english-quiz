📚 오늘의 학습 카드 (풀기 전에 읽기)

1. 들은 말 전달하기 S5

A said (that) + 주어 + would + 동사원형 현재→과거로 한 칸 이동: will→would · is→was
  • The dentist said the treatment would take about a month.
  • My coworker said she would send me the file by noon.
  • I heard the cafe downstairs is closing next month. (heard로 전할 때)
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수 — “house owner says air conditionor change will be next month” → said 뒤에서는 will이 아니라 would. / “my friend say she make me meeting” → say에 시제가 없고, make도 would + 동사원형이어야 해요.

2. 전치사 뒤에는 무조건 -ing S6

about / of / in / without / after / for + 동사-ing
  • I'm worried about making a mistake in the interview.
  • Thanks for inviting me to the party.
  • You can't pass the test without studying.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수 — “it's dosen't work about talk with him” → “~해봐야 소용없다”는 There's no point in + -ing 한 덩어리로. 전치사 뒤 동사원형은 금지!

3. ~할까 하다가 → 결국 / 점점 ~하게 됐다 S22

was thinking about + -ing ~할까 하다가 ended up + -ing 결국 ~했다 have come to + 동사원형 (시간이 지나) ~하게 됐다
  • I was thinking about getting bangs, but I ended up just getting a trim.
  • After a few weeks, I've come to like the new office.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수 — “i thinging of getting taxi, but i work on my way to home” → 결말은 ended up + -ing. / “since i living here, i like silent more and more” → After living here for a while, I've come to ~ 패턴으로.

4. 단어 선택 함정 5종 S12

노트북 = laptop (notebook은 공책!) 집주인 = landlord 부장님 = manager 세탁소 = the dry cleaners 가사 = lyrics
  • My laptop battery dies so fast these days.
  • I asked my landlord if I could keep a cat.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수 — “my new notebook is not working”(notebook=공책), “house owner”(→landlord), “laundery shop”(→the dry cleaners), “i didn't know words”(가사→lyrics).
🔊 발음·연음 노트 (채점 없음 — 소리 내서 3번씩)
said he'd[쎄디드]said의 d에 he가 붙으며 h가 사라지고, he'd는 [이드]로 약해져요
ended up[엔디덥]d가 뒤 모음 u에 붙어 한 단어처럼 굴러가요
no point in[노 포이닌]point의 t가 탈락하고 n이 in과 이어져요
about it[어바우릿]t가 모음 사이에 끼면 ㄹ 소리로 굴러가요
let me know[렘미 노우]t가 m 앞에서 삼켜져 거의 안 들려요
sign up for[싸이넙 포]n이 up에 붙고, for는 약하게 [퍼/포]
