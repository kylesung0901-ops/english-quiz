1. 후회·가정은 조동사 세트로 — 시제는 조동사에 싣는다 S21S3

~했어야 했는데 should've + p.p. ~하지 말걸 shouldn't have + p.p. ~할 수도 있었는데 could've + p.p. ~했으면 …했을 텐데 If I had p.p., I would've p.p. ※ should + 과거동사(should took) 조합은 영어에 없음
  • I should've checked the weather before leaving.
  • If I had taken the earlier flight, I would've made it to the wedding.
⚠️ 이렇게 쓰면 ❌ → 이렇게 ⭕ (어제 카일 작문)
❌ i shoud took the picture
⭕ I should've taken more pictures.
should've + p.p.
❌ i should reduse my baggage
⭕ I should've packed lighter.
후회 = should've / 짐 줄이기 = pack lighter
❌ if i exchange the monye in advance, i can safe the exchange fee
⭕ If I had exchanged my money in advance, I would've saved on the exchange fees.
had p.p. → would've p.p. / save(동사)·safe(형용사)

2. 신호 단어부터 문두에 — I heard / At first S29S27

"~라던데요·~한대요" → 문장 맨 앞 I heard ~ "처음엔 ~였는데" → At first, 주어 + 과거동사 "할수록 점점" → the more ~, the more ~
  • I heard that new gym is open 24 hours.
  • At first, the guitar class was so hard that I almost gave up, but the more I practiced, the more confident I got.
⚠️ 이렇게 쓰면 ❌ → 이렇게 ⭕ (어제 카일 작문)
❌ this winter not too much cold
⭕ I heard this winter isn't as cold as people expected.
I heard 문두 + be동사 필수
❌ frist time i can't eat that, because so spicy, but i can suck it up
⭕ At first, it was so spicy that I couldn't eat it, but the more I ate it, the more I liked it.
At first / 과거는 couldn't

3. 동사는 전치사까지가 한 단어 S6

work for + 회사 think about + -ing wait for ~ talk to ~ after + -ing save on + 비용
  • She works for a game company.
  • I'll text you after getting home.
⚠️ 이렇게 쓰면 ❌ → 이렇게 ⭕ (어제 실제 대화에서)
❌ I work in the airline company
⭕ I work for an airline.
회사 다니다 = work for
❌ i thought waiting bus
⭕ I thought about waiting for the bus.
about + for 둘 다 필수
❌ I will go there after finish my work
⭕ I'll go there after I finish work.
after -ing / after 주어+동사
