가정법 과거완료 wouldn't have had to 혼합 가정법 시간·거리

오늘의 학습 카드 — 이 4개만 보고 들어가세요

구조 1 · 가정법 과거완료 — 결과가 <과거>

If + 주어 + had p.p. , 주어 + would have p.p. 부정형 → 주어 + wouldn't have p.p.
  • If you had called me in advance, I would have told you.
    미리 전화했더라면, 내가 알려줬을 텐데.
  • If I had charged my phone, I wouldn't have gotten lost.
    폰을 충전했더라면, 길을 잃지 않았을 텐데.
  • If I had used a little less salt, this soup would've tasted much better.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수
· if i booking ticket early… → if절은 had + p.p.가 통째로 들어갑니다. -ing나 현재형이 아닙니다.
· would have prepare / will have prepared → would 뒤는 have + p.p. 고정. will은 절대 안 됩니다.

구조 2 · "~안 해도 됐을 텐데" — wouldn't have had to

If + 주어 + had p.p. , 주어 + wouldn't have had to + 동사원형 ↑have ↑had ↑to (셋 다 필요)
  • If I'd booked my ticket in advance, I wouldn't have had to pay this much.
    미리 예매했더라면, 이렇게 많이 안 내도 됐을 텐데.
  • If I hadn't missed the last train, I wouldn't have had to pay for a taxi.
  • If I had gone to the gym in the morning, I wouldn't have had to go in the afternoon.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수
· i dont have to pay → 과거에 안 일어난 일이라 현재형이 아니라 wouldn't have had to pay.
· wouldn't had to / wouldn't have to → have와 had가 둘 다 있어야 "그때 안 해도 됐다"가 됩니다. to 뒤는 항상 동사원형.

구조 3 · 혼합 가정법 — 결과가 <지금>

If + 주어 + had p.p. , 주어 + would/could + 동사원형 ( + now / by now ) ↑ have 없음!

판별법: 문장 끝에 now / by now가 붙으면 → 주절에서 have를 뺍니다.

  • If I had taken my medicine on time, I wouldn't be feeling this sick now.
  • If I hadn't deleted that picture, I could show it to you now.
  • If I hadn't sold that company's stock, I'd be pretty rich by now.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수
· if i learn the piano when i was young, right now i good at it → if절엔 had learned, 주절엔 would be. 동사를 빠뜨리지 마세요.
· I could have shown it to you now → now가 있는데 have를 넣으면 시점이 과거로 밀립니다. could show.

구조 4 · 시간·거리 표현

It takes about 5 minutes on foot. (foot 단수, on) It takes about 20 minutes by taxi. (교통수단 = by + 무관사) a five-minute walk (하이픈 붙으면 minute 단수)
  • How long does it take from here? — It takes about 5 minutes on foot.
  • The company I work for is a five-minute walk from the subway station.
  • It's 10 minutes on foot.
⚠️ 자주 하는 실수
· five-minutes walk → 하이픈으로 형용사가 되면 단수: a five-minute walk. 관사 a도 필수.
· by foot / on feet → 걸어서는 on foot 하나로 외웁니다.
